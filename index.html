A Decade of Integration & Impact

For the past ten years, research, teaching, and service have been woven together around one guiding question: how can we increase access, equity, and participation in STEM? That work spans course-based undergraduate research experiences (CUREs), guided-inquiry pedagogy, tiered mentoring models, and studies of belonging among undergraduate and graduate students in the life sciences.

What This Work Is About

Three interconnected threads anchor the lab's research program.

Belonging

Every project is anchored by the study of belonging — the subjective sense of connection and legitimacy students feel as members of the STEM community — and how it shapes learning, persistence, and identity.

Pedagogical Innovation

Development and evaluation of novel guided-inquiry pedagogies that pair active learning with tactile teaching tools, case studies, and digital manipulatives across diverse institutions and STEM fields.

Programmatic Structures

Direct comparisons of high-impact STEM experiences — stand-alone vs. linked CUREs, and traditional mentoring vs. Interdisciplinary Tiered Mentoring (ITM) — to understand what structures best serve students.
