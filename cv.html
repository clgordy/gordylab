Research Directions

Interests in both Scholarship of Teaching and Learning (SOTL) and Discipline-Based Education Research (DBER) are focused on equity, access, and belonging in STEM. SOTL work addresses these questions within individual classrooms by evaluating the impact of targeted interventions — many driven by the interests of undergraduate and graduate education research mentees. These projects span undergraduate and graduate courses, online and in-person classes, and include interventions pairing Guided Inquiry Learning with Tactile Teaching Tools, case studies, or digital manipulatives; co-creation of student-driven case studies; strategies for guiding undergraduates in reading primary literature; replacement of exams with concept maps; strategies for communication between online and in-person students; and adaptation of K-12 educational technologies for the college classroom.

DBER work addresses equity, access, and belonging at a larger scale through studies designed to generate and test hypotheses and disseminate generalizable findings that reach beyond individual classrooms — carried out collaboratively with colleagues in Biological Sciences and the Genetics and Genomics Academy, with the interdisciplinary Biological Sciences Belonging Research Group (founded 2022), with participants in the NSF Research Coordination Network in Undergraduate Biology Education, and with independent research mentees.
